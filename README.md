# wristport

**Give your Galaxy Watch a second life.**

**wristport** is a modern, dark-themed desktop application designed to keep Tizen-based Samsung Galaxy Watches alive and useful[cite: 1]. It provides an intuitive interface to browse the Tizen Archive and wirelessly sideload legacy apps and watch faces directly onto your watch using Smart Development Bridge (`sdb.exe`)[cite: 1].

---

### ✨ Key Features

- **Curated Tizen Archive Browser:** Search and explore 700+ preserved Tizen apps and watch faces with instant search (`Ctrl+K` / `⌘K`)[cite: 1].
- **Wireless SDB Sideloading:** Automatically scan local network ports (`26101`) or connect directly via your watch's local IP address—no USB cables required[cite: 1].
- **Built-in SDB Management:** Configure, verify, and swap your `sdb.exe` binary right from the dashboard[cite: 1].
- **App & Activity Tracking:** View installed watch packages and monitor sideloading activity in one clean sidebar[cite: 1].
- **Guided Setup Flow:** Includes a step-by-step beginner guide and troubleshooting instructions for enabling Watch Debugging and pairing over Wi-Fi[cite: 1].

### 🚀 Quick Start (How It Works)

1. **Configure SDB:** Point wristport to your `sdb.exe` executable[cite: 1].
2. **Enable Debugging:** Turn on **Debugging** and **Developer Options** on your Galaxy Watch[cite: 1].
3. **Pair Over Wi-Fi:** Connect both your PC and watch to the same Wi-Fi network and scan/connect on port `26101`[cite: 1].
4. **Install:** Pick an app or `.tpk`/`.wgt` package from the library and deploy it straight to your wrist[cite: 1].
